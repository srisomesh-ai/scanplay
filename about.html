About ScanPlay

ScanPlay is an interactive media platform that turns ordinary printed material into playable video experiences.

Today a poster, a newspaper advertisement, a product package, a visiting card or a wedding invitation is static. ScanPlay connects that physical print to video: point a smartphone at the picture and the video plays right on it — in the phone's browser, with no app to install.

A business uploads its existing design and a video. From that moment every printed copy of that design is interactive: the customer scans it and instantly sees the video, offer, demonstration or message. Every scan is counted, so print becomes measurable for the first time.

Our vision is to make every physical advertisement, product and communication capable of becoming interactive.

ScanPlay creates value for everyone in the chain
  • Businesses and brands deliver richer content on the same print space and measure real engagement.
  • Customers get instant access to videos, offers, demonstrations and experiences — just by pointing their phone.
  • Distributors and promoters — photographers, event managers, print shops — bring the technology to businesses in their city, building a scalable local network.

ScanPlay is built and hosted in India by ScanPlay LLP, Visakhapatnam. We are building the bridge between offline media and digital content, making traditional advertising more engaging, measurable and interactive.
