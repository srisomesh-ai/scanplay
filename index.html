How it works

Three steps. One QR.

Everything happens in your ScanPlay studio, from your phone or laptop. The same QR keeps working as you add more pictures later.

01

Upload the picture & video

Any photo, artwork, invitation or label. Any video — a message, a walkthrough, a reel. Crop to the printed area.

02

Link & get your QR

The studio analyses the picture, pairs it with the video and gives you a QR to download and print.

03

Scan, point, play

Anyone scans the QR, the camera opens, recognises the print, and the video plays locked to it — edge to edge, with sound.
