FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do guests need to install an app?

No. Scanning the QR opens the player in the phone's browser. It works on iPhone and Android.

How is this different from a QR that just opens a video?

With ScanPlay the video plays on top of the printed picture and moves with it — the picture itself comes alive. A normal QR only opens a video page.

What kind of pictures work best?

Photos and artwork with detail and contrast: portraits, posters, packaging art. Plain logos, solid colours or very glossy prints track less reliably. The studio warns you if a picture is weak.

Can I add more pictures later without reprinting the QR?

Yes. One QR per account recognises every picture in that account. Add or replace pictures any time; the printed QR never changes.

How big should the print be?

Anything from a 4×6 photo to a hoarding. The phone just needs to see the whole picture clearly.

Does the video have sound?

Yes. Phones start videos muted until the user taps the screen once; a "tap for sound" chip makes that obvious.
