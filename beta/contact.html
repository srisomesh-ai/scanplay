About ScanPlay

ScanPlay is a web-based augmented-reality service that lets anyone make a printed photo play a video. Customers upload a photo and a video in the ScanPlay Studio, receive a QR code to print, and viewers scan the QR and point their phone at the print to watch the video play on it — no app installation required. ScanPlay is built and operated in Visakhapatnam, India.
